
A complete **Data Analytics Project** built using **SQL Server** and **Power BI** to analyze gym membership data, customer behavior, attendance patterns, revenue trends, and membership retention. This project demonstrates how data can be transformed into actionable business insights through SQL queries and interactive dashboards.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The objective of this project is to analyze gym membership data to answer real-world business questions such as:

- Which membership plan generates the highest revenue?
- Which customers are the most active?
- Which cities contribute the most revenue?
- What are the busiest gym hours?
- Which payment mode is preferred by customers?
- How effective is customer renewal and retention?

The project includes SQL-based analysis and an interactive Power BI dashboard for business reporting.

## 📋 Key Business Insights

- Annual memberships generate the highest revenue.
- Monthly memberships have the lowest renewal rate.
- Customers aged 26–35 form the largest member group.
- Evening hours record the highest attendance.
- Digital payment methods are the most preferred.
- A small group of loyal members accounts for a significant number of gym visits.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Promote Annual memberships through discounts.
- Launch loyalty programs for highly active members.
- Improve customer retention with renewal reminders.
- Increase staff availability during peak hours.
- Expand marketing in high-performing cities.
- Encourage digital payment options.
